This tool does not diagnose diabetes and does not give medical advice. Only a blood test (HbA1c or fasting glucose) can show if you have prediabetes or diabetes.

DiaRisk-5 points come from a logistic regression model trained on 3,669 US adults who were never told they have diabetes (NHANES 2021–2023). Test-set AUC: 0.75 for HbA1c ≥ 5.7% and 0.79 for undiagnosed diabetes. Group 4: Baran Toros, Berrin Şan, Baran Can Zöngür · GitHub repository
